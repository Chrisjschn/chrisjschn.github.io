Privacy Policy

CPA AUD Blitz: Exam Prep · Effective October 31, 2026

AUD Blitz collects no personal data. No accounts, no ads, no tracking. It sends anonymous usage statistics, described below, which you can switch off.

What the app stores

Your quiz progress — answers, scores, XP, streaks, and settings — is stored only on your device. It never reaches our servers, because there are no servers: the app works fully offline.

Anonymous usage statistics

AUD Blitz sends anonymous usage statistics to TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service in Germany, so we can see which study modes get used and which questions people miss. It is always of these kinds: counts of how the app is used (quizzes started, finished and left, with their mode and score band), the numbers of questions that were missed, levels and trophies reached, which settings were switched, whether a backup was used, the platform (iPhone, iPad, web), and a random install code that is hashed on your device before it leaves and hashed again on arrival, so it cannot be traced back to you or your device. Not sent: your name, email, contacts, location, device identifiers, your answers, or anything you type. Nothing is used for advertising, and nothing is shared with anyone else. We may add more counts of these kinds; we will not add anything that identifies you without changing this policy and its date.

To stop it, open the app's Settings and switch off Anonymous usage stats. Nothing is sent while it is off.

Optional iCloud backup

If you turn on iCloud sync in the app's Settings, your progress is backed up to your personal iCloud account using Apple's iCloud key-value storage, and synced between your own devices. That data lives in your private iCloud storage, is governed by Apple's privacy policy, and is never visible to us. Turn the toggle off at any time to stop syncing.

Notifications

The optional daily study reminder is scheduled locally on your device. Nothing is sent to or from a server.

Data deletion

Use Reset all progress in the app's Settings to erase everything the app stores, or simply delete the app. If iCloud sync was on, turning it off and resetting removes the app's data from your device; you can also manage app data in your iCloud settings.

Children

The app is a professional exam study tool and collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes & contact

If this policy ever changes, the update will appear on this page with a new effective date. Questions: blitzgamelearning@gmail.com.

AUD Blitz is an independent study tool for the Auditing and Attestation (AUD) section of the CPA exam. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the AICPA or NASBA.
