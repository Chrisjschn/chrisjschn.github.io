Privacy Policy

the web-only Blitz apps · Effective October 31, 2026

QB Blitz, 1041 Blitz, Dealers and Recon Blitz collects no personal data. No accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking, no third-party services.

What the app stores

Your quiz progress — answers, scores, XP, streaks, and settings — is stored only on your device. It never reaches our servers, because there are no servers: the app works fully offline.

Data deletion

Use Reset all progress in the app's Settings to erase everything the app stores, or simply delete the app or clear your browser's site data.

Children

The apps are professional exam study tools and collect no personal data from anyone, including children.
