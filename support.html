How do I back up my progress?

Tap the gear (⚙️) at the top of the home screen to open Settings. The fastest way is the iCloud sync toggle — flip it on once and progress backs up automatically and syncs to your other devices. You can also save a backup file or copy a backup code from the same screen, and restore it on any device (restores merge with what's there — they never erase progress).

Why didn't I get my daily reminder?

Check that the reminder toggle is on in the app's Settings (the gear at the top of the home screen), and that notifications are allowed for the app in iOS Settings. Reminders are scheduled on your device, so they work without any internet connection.

How do streaks and freezes work?

Answering at least one question a day keeps your streak alive. You earn streak freezes as you play (up to 2); a freeze is spent automatically to protect your streak on a day you miss.

How does Weak Spots pick questions?

It rebuilds quizzes from the questions you've missed most, and pays double XP when you get a previously-missed question right.

Does the app work offline?

Yes — everything works with no connection: quizzes, progress, streaks, and reminders. iCloud backup syncs the next time you're online.

How do I start over?

Settings → Reset all progress erases everything on the device.
